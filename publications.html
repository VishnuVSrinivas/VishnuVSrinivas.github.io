Mechanistic Interpretability of Semantic Abstraction in Biomedical Texts NeurIPS 2025 FM4LS Workshop

2025

Co-first author

Probes how biomedical language models represent the same content in technical and plain-language text. Encoder models converge to shared representations in mid-to-late layers (CKA above 0.85 in BioBERT and SciBERT), while BioGPT stays more sensitive to register. Activation patching shows attention heads, not MLPs, drive this invariance.
