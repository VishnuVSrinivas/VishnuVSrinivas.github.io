Mechanistic Interpretability of Semantic Abstraction in Biomedical Texts NeurIPS 2025 FM4LS Workshop

2025

Nikhil Gourisetty*, Snata Mohanty, Vishnu Srinivas*, Soumil Jain, Sunith Vallabhaneni, Kevin Zhu, Sunishchal Dev (*equal contribution)

Probes BioBERT, SciBERT, Clinical-T5, and BioGPT on aligned technical and plain-language biomedical abstracts to test whether they form register-invariant semantic representations. Encoder models converge to shared semantic states in mid-to-late layers, with CKA above 0.85 in BioBERT and SciBERT layers 8–12 and convergence in Clinical-T5 encoder layers 6–10; BioGPT stabilizes later and is more sensitive to register (mean Cohen’s d around 0.22). Activation patching shows attention heads, not MLPs, are the main causal mediators of this invariance, strongest in T5 (Cohen’s d = 0.33).
